Done the Reading

History, with the footnotes left in.

Done the Reading is a YouTube channel of explainer videos about history: the real story behind the version everyone half-remembers. Every video is researched from cited sources before a word of the script is written. If you’d like the sources for a video, just ask.

The tone is a curious friend who has done the reading, not a lecturer. Expect concrete examples, honest uncertainty where historians disagree, and the occasional joke.

Watch

New videos are on the channel: youtube.com/@robertclancy1691. Start with Who Was the First Samurai.

How the videos are made

Scripts, narration and animation are produced with the help of AI tools, and every video is reviewed by a person before it's published. Where narration uses a synthetic or cloned voice, the video is labelled with YouTube’s altered or synthetic content disclosure.

Videos are uploaded to the channel with a small personal tool, Done the Reading Studio, that runs on the channel owner’s own computer and uses the YouTube API Services. How it handles data is set out in the privacy policy.
